St. Paul College of Makati (SPCM) is strategically located in the vibrant heart of Makati City, adjacent to the historic Sts. Peter and Paul Parish. As a distinguished educational institution, SPCM is committed to delivering high-quality education and promoting the holistic development of its students. Our mission is to nurture not only academic excellence but also character formation, critical thinking, and social responsibility, preparing our students to become well-rounded individuals ready to face the challenges of the future.


We offer a wide range of programs designed to cater to the diverse needs and interests of our student body, including various academic tracks, extracurricular activities, and community engagement initiatives. Our faculty members are dedicated professionals who strive to create an inclusive and supportive learning environment, ensuring that each student receives the guidance and resources they need to succeed.


Our office hours are Monday to Friday from 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM. We warmly invite you to visit us during these hours for any inquiries, enrollment assistance, or to learn more about our programs and the vibrant community at SPCP. We look forward to welcoming you and helping you embark on an enriching educational journey.
