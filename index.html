Shaping Paulinians Towards Compassionate Mission

St. Paul College of Makati, a Catholic Paulinian educational institution, believes that every child can best learn within a caring community, and endeavors to provide meaningful, relevant, and engaging learning environment where the potentials and gifts of the child are developed to the fullest towards the formation of an integrated, Christ-centered, and socially-responsive Paulinian.

PROGRAM OFFERINGS

Early Years

Our Preschool Program provides a nurturing, caring and God loving environment that promotes the development of a healthy self-concept and a positive self-esteem among children.

Grade School

The Grade School Academic Program is a six - year program that aims to form Academically Excellent, Morally Upright, and Socially Responsible Paulinians.

High School

The Junior High School Program aims to form integrated Paulinians who are imbued with Christian Catholic values coupled with a solid grounding on the 21st century skills in their academic formation.

Senior High School

The Senior High School Program aims to hone and develop the interest and expertise of Paulinians in the various fields of study and prepare them to better tackle the rigors of college life and the world of work.

Principal's Welcome

WELCOME TO ST. PAUL COLLEGE OF MAKATI!


A Legacy of Faith, Excellence, and Service Since 1942


Warmest greetings and welcome to St. Paul College of Makati (SPCM)!


Rooted in a rich legacy of Catholic education and guided by the charism of the Sisters of St. Paul of Chartres, St. Paul College of Makati has been faithfully committed to forming individuals who excel not only in knowledge and skills but, more improtantly, in character, faith, and service.


Since its humble beginnings in 1942, our institution has remained steadfast in its mission of providing quality, Christ-centered education that nurtures the intellectual, spiritual, moral, social, and emotional dimensions of every learner. Situated in the heart of Makati City, beside the historic Saints Peter and Paul Parish, SPCM continues to be a vibrants community where faith is lived, knowledge is pursued, values are strengthened, and meaningful relationships are nurtured.


We believe that educations goes beyond academic achievement. It is a transformative journey of discovering one's gifts, developing one's potential, cultivating a compassionate heart, and embracing the responsibility of making a positive difference in society. Through our dedicated faculty and personal, engaging learning experiences, diverse academic programs, and meaningful community involvement, we strive to provide an environment where every student is encouraged to grow, excel, and become the person God calls them to be.


As we embrace the opportunities and challenges of an ever-changing world, we remain anchored in our Catholic identity and Paulinian values, inspired by the spirit of "Christ-centeredness, Charity, and Commitment." We envision our learners becoming competent, compassionate, responsible, and faith-filled individuals who will contribute meaningfully to the Church, the nation, and the global community.


To our students, parents, alumni, partners, and visitors, thank you for being part of thr SPCM family. May this webpage serve as a window into our vibrant school community and a reflection of the values, aspirations, and enduring commitment that define us.


At St. Paul College of Makati, we do not simply educate minds; we form hearts, nurture faith, inspire excellence, and empover lives for service.


Once again, welcome to St. Paul College of Makati, where every learner is valued, every potential is nurtured, and every journey is inspired by faith and love.


God bless us all!

- Sr. Nilda Caparanga, SPC
