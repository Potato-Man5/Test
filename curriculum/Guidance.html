GUIDANCE CENTER

RATIONALE

The Guidance Center operates as a service arm of the School. The services extended primarily to the pupils/students. It also opens its doors to our adult community: parents, faculty, administrators, and personnel. SPCM alumni may also avail of the services. The Center aims to assist its clients whether individually or in groups towards enchanced self-understanding and interpersonal relationships.


The Guidance Program, which are preventive and developmental in nature, are integral to the entire educational process. They are intimately connected with the curricular and co-curricular programs, geared towards the implementation and realization of the thrust of the Institution. The goals and objectives of the Guidance Center contribute to the learners' growing awareness and understanding of his/her developing self-concept, attitudes and values, psychological, emotional, social and moral maturity, for optimum development and achievement as a unique person.


OBJECTIVES

The main goal of the Guidance Center is to carry out the obligation of the School in the proper molding of the personality of each student in order that he/she is helped to:


a. Be better aware of and understands his/her individual potentials;


b. Handle or deal with different kinds of problems;


c. Make their own choices – such that his/her life is a testament of his/her Catholic faith, consistent of his/her moral values, and recognizes the worth and effects to other people;
